About the studio

Created in the Delta. Built for bigger stories.

Delta House Productions is a creative production company based in the Mississippi Delta. We develop original media and help local people, businesses, and communities tell their stories professionally.

Delta House Productions is part of Herrin Enterprises and works alongside Living in the Delta and Herrin Photography while maintaining its own production identity.
